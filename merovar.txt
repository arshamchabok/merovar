Merovar

Merovar is the largest central continent and the political heart of the known world. It contains broad plains, dense forests, mountain ranges, river valleys, lakes, and long coastlines suited to farming, trade, and warfare. Its many regions are culturally distinct, but they share a common Merovan identity and see their continent as the centre of civilization.

Merovar is home to farmers, merchants, soldiers, knights, craftsmen, priests, scholars, sailors, and powerful landowners. Most of its population lives in fertile riverlands and around major roads, ports, castles, and market towns. Its people are called Merovans.

The Aldermarch

The Aldermarch is the mountainous land northwest of Merovar. It is a cold, rugged country of steep valleys, rocky coastlines, high pastures, fortified towns, and isolated stone strongholds. Travel is difficult, and many communities are separated by mountains, forests, and narrow passes.

Its people are known for discipline, personal honour, military service, and strict codes of conduct. Knighthood is highly respected, and even ordinary villagers take pride in loyalty, courage, and keeping their word. The population consists largely of shepherds, miners, hunters, soldiers, craftsmen, and mounted warriors. They are generally called Marchers.

Amberfold

Amberfold is the fertile southwestern continent. Its landscape is made up of wide rivers, green fields, orchards, vineyards, flowered hills, and sheltered valleys. The climate is mild, and the soil produces large quantities of grain, fruit, wine, vegetables, and livestock.

Amberfold is densely populated compared with the harsher lands around it. Its people include farmers, vintners, millers, horse breeders, merchants, cooks, artists, and skilled craftsmen. Its towns are wealthy and colourful, and its people are known for enjoying food, festivals, music, fashion, and comfortable living. They are commonly called Amberfolk or simply the people of the Fold.

The Skerrows

The Skerrows are a harsh southern land formed from rocky islands, narrow channels, cliffs, and storm-beaten shores. There is little good farmland, but the islands possess deep natural harbours, strong timber, iron deposits, and easy access to major sea routes.

The Skerrowborn are the finest shipbuilders, navigators, sailors, and naval fighters in the known world. Their communities are built around harbours, shipyards, fishing villages, mines, and coastal fortresses. Most people learn to sail while young, and a person’s skill at sea matters more than wealth or elegance. They are called Skerrowborn, while outsiders sometimes call them Stoneborn.

The Tatters

The Tatters are a scattered archipelago between Merovar and Kadresh. The islands vary greatly: some are barren rocks, while others contain hidden coves, freshwater springs, fishing settlements, jungle-covered hills, and crowded free ports.

The Tatters are inhabited by pirates, smugglers, escaped prisoners, exiled nobles, mercenaries, fishermen, traders, and people seeking lives beyond the laws of the great continents. Their ports are dangerous but culturally diverse, with languages, religions, weapons, and goods arriving from every shore. The inhabitants are known as Tatterfolk, islanders, or the Free Crews, depending on where they live.

Kadresh

Kadresh is the vast eastern continent. Its interior contains deserts, salt flats, dry mountains, and open steppes, while its rivers and coastal regions support fertile farmland, gardens, forests, and enormous cities. The land is much larger and more culturally varied than any single western realm.

Kadresh is home to desert riders, river farmers, caravan merchants, sailors, craftsmen, scholars, soldiers, nomadic clans, and the inhabitants of wealthy city-states. Some communities live in crowded coastal cities, while others travel between distant wells and seasonal grazing lands. The people are broadly called Kadreshi, although most identify more strongly with their own city, region, language, or tribe.
